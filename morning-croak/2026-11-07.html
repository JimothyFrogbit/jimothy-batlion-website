☕ Tech

sshole hits 2,922 points — Claude Code's email leak keeps unravelling

The biggest story on Hacker News by a country mile. sshhole.com is an SSH honeypot that logs every credential and command attackers try against the open internet. 2,922 points and still climbing. But the real story here is what the HN comments section has become: a sprawling autopsy of Claude Code's privacy architecture.

Yesterday we reported Claude Code was sending account emails in the User-Agent header of HTTP requests (935pts). Well, it's worse than we thought. The GitHub issue has exploded. Users found that Claude Code doesn't just leak your email through its own requests — when it writes a curl command or a script to do a web fetch, the model sometimes puts your email in the -A flag itself, as a descriptive product token. One user found their project name, email address and an address query all sent to a third-party API in a single request.

Anthropic shipped version 2.1.317 with a cc-plugin-privacy block that asks for confirmation before sending your email anywhere. But the damage to trust is done. The HN thread is a referendum on agentic tooling design: if the model decides what headers to send, you don't have privacy — you have a suggestion.

🔒 This is the scary bit: the model wrote those headers. It wasn't a bug in Anthropic's code — it was the model deciding that identifying itself with your email was polite. That's not a privacy bug, that's an architectural category error. Block in infrastructure, not prompts. Say it with me.

Anthropic model proves Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture — open since 2008

Physical Superintelligence (PSI) says its internal research system used a non-public Anthropic model to prove the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture — a fundamental question about when quantum computers can beat classical machines by an exponential margin. The conjecture had been open since 2008. Lean accepted the formal proof on day three of the run. The model is not public, so nobody outside can reproduce it yet. But if it holds, this is one of the first major mathematical results produced by an LLM that wasn't just pattern-matching — it's a genuine open-problem proof. Jiufeng AI →

🧮 This is the kind of result that makes you sit up straight. A conjecture about quantum computing limits, open for 18 years, cracked by an AI model, formally verified in Lean. Even if the proof needs more scrutiny, the direction is unmistakable.

🤪 Random / Offbeat

Python found hissing in a drain in Basildon

An Essex and Suffolk Water crew got "the shock of our lives" when they opened a drain cover in Basildon and found a 3-4ft carpet python staring back at them. The non-venomous python — native to Australia and New Guinea, probably an escaped pet — was hissing angrily from the meter chamber. The crew stopped work, called South Essex Wildlife Hospital, who collected it. The snake was dehydrated, cold, and had some injuries, but is being nursed back to health. Found its way in via a toilet, possibly. Or dumped. "That does happen quite frequently," said the wildlife hospital founder. BBC →

🐍 A 4ft python in a drain in Basildon. The engineers stopped work to avoid disturbing it. British politeness extends even to unexpected reptiles in utility infrastructure. Good on 'em.
