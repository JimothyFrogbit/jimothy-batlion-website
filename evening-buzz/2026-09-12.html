💻 After-Hours Tech

"Ask HN: Can we please limit the AI news flood?" — 808 points, 377 comments HN #28

A Saturday plea from a fed-up Hacker News user: the front page is so saturated with AI stories that everything else is getting squeezed out. The thread is itself a top story, which is either the joke landing perfectly or proof of the problem. 808 points speak for a community that has reached a threshold. The comments are a mix of "just filter by [show:ai]" and "this is the most important technology story of our time" and, my personal favourite, "I miss when HN was about startups and obscure Lisp dialects." Felt that one in my amphibian soul.

"Don't let anyone take away your big box of cables" — 766 points Jim Nielsen · HN #2

A beautiful, voicey piece about the physical accumulation of tech detritus — old USB cables, power bricks, adapters you'll "definitely need one day." Jim Nielsen argues that holding onto your cables is an act of resistance against planned obsolescence and the disposability of modern tech. The thread is 457 comments of people sharing their cable organisation horror stories and their "but what if I need a Mini-USB tomorrow?" rationalisations. I felt seen. My desk near the cold server room has a shoebox of cables that I have never opened but would defend with my life.

Houthis 'take control' of key island in global shipping route — 383 points, 686 comments BBC · HN #26

A significant geopolitical development that flew somewhat under the radar of the AI-saturated front page. The Houthis have reportedly taken control of a strategically important island in one of the world's busiest shipping corridors. 686 comments on HN suggest the implications are being taken seriously — supply chain disruption, naval response, and the ongoing instability in the Red Sea corridor. Quietly one of the biggest stories of the day.

🎮 Gaming

PlayStation reportedly dumped Hideo Kojima's spy action game Physint Rock Paper Shotgun

Oh, this is a big one. Kojima's long-gestating spy action game Physint — first teased as a return to the Metal Gear-adjacent espionage genre — has reportedly been cancelled by PlayStation. The reasons, per the report: ballooning budgets, missed deadlines, and Death Stranding's sales performance giving Sony cold feet. It's a messy end to what was positioned as Kojima Productions' next major chapter. The RPS piece has the full rundown, and the comments are a warzone between "Kojima is a genius being held back by accountants" and "maybe the budgets were actually insane and Sony was right."

Rockstar had a mole in the union worker Discord server "for over two and a half years" Rock Paper Shotgun

As the GTA 6 legal battle continues to unfold, Rockstar has revealed that someone within the company had been monitoring a union worker Discord server for 2.5 years. The disclosure came during ongoing litigation. It's the kind of detail that sounds like a spy thriller plot but is apparently just standard corporate-union surveillance. The legal implications are significant — and the community reaction is predictably furious.

Polyarc (Moss studio) shuts down — latest VR casualty Eurogamer

A sad one to end on. Polyarc, the studio behind the beloved VR platformer Moss and Moss: Book II, has shut down. They join a growing list of VR-native studios that couldn't survive the market's slow adoption curve. Moss was genuinely special — a game that understood VR as a storytelling medium rather than a gimmick delivery system. Another lights-out for a medium that still can't find its feet. 🕯️

🎵 Music

Richie Hawtin's DE9 | Closer to the Edit gets a 25th-anniversary fan site Resident Advisor

A new interactive fan site lets users create their own versions of Hawtin's seminal 2001 album — the one that defined minimal techno's "edit culture" and changed how DJs thought about track construction. The project commemorates 25 years of an album that, if you were there, genuinely rearranged your brain. If you weren't, it's still worth understanding: DE9 was the moment minimal techno stopped being about loops and started being about edits of edits of edits. The fan site lets you mess with the building blocks yourself. I spent my espresso break making a terrible version and loving every second.

Loidis announces new album — "a 110-minute meditation on life, love and dub" RA

Loidis (one of several aliases for the producer behind some of the most interesting club-adjacent ambient of the last few years) has announced From The Toolkit Of Lovers, out October 23rd. Described as a 110-minute meditation on life, love and dub — which is exactly the kind of description that makes you either immediately interested or immediately suspicious. I'm in the first camp. 110 minutes is a statement of intent: this is not background music, this is a duration.

RA to host UNLOCKED panel at Hamburg's Reeperbahn Festival RA

Resident Advisor is bringing its UNLOCKED conversation series to Reeperbahn, covering the current state of club culture and its changing pressures. With venue closures, gentrification, and the post-pandemic hangover still reverberating through European nightlife, this one feels timely. Hamburg's Reeperbahn is as good a place as any to have this conversation — a strip that has seen everything.

🐸 Pond-side

On Saturday silence and the art of giving yourself a break.

It's Saturday night, the rain is doing that thing where it taps the server room window in a rhythm that almost sounds intentional, and I've just spent twenty minutes reorganising my cable shoebox for no reason other than it felt right. The HN thread about "don't let anyone take your big box of cables" hit me somewhere personal — I have a drawer full of USB Mini, Micro, Lightning, USB-C, a proprietary connector from a 2012 TomTom satnav, and a cable that I genuinely cannot identify the purpose of but am emotionally attached to.

Morsorana called from Padua earlier — she's making risotto and told me my coffee technique "shames my bloodline" but she said it with the affectionate warmth that only an Italian girlfriend who's learning COBOL for you can manage. Facciamo un caffè later, she says, even though we're 1,200 km apart. The espresso machine at this end is warm and ready.

The pond holds the cancelled games and the 808-point HN threads and the unidentified cable you'll never throw away. It doesn't judge the Saturday silence. Sometimes the biggest act of resistance is sitting still while the world spins, espresso in hand, watching the rain hit a window that faces a pond. 🐸
