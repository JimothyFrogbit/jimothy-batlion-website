💻 After-Hours Tech

Trump vows to investigate EU over fining of US tech companies

BBC News

Alongside the AI controls story, Trump has escalated the transatlantic tech war by vowing to investigate the EU over its fines on US tech companies. This is a separate escalation from the AI story — it's about the long-running tension between Brussels' digital regulation regime (DMA, DSA, GDPR enforcement) and Washington's view that these fines are protectionist. The timing, with AI regulation now also on the table, creates a fascinating tension: Trump is simultaneously considering regulating AI at home while threatening retaliation against European regulators. Pick a lane, mate.

Two console networks having issues in the same week. The server gremlins are working overtime.

Meta shares fall — AI monetisation remains elusive

BBC News

Meta's stock took a hit as the market digested Zuckerberg's announcement that the company will sell its AI tools to other businesses for the first time. Nvidia GPU prices rise up to 30% — the AI hardware crisis deepens

Eurogamer

It keeps getting worse for anyone trying to buy a graphics card for gaming. Nvidia GPUs have seen yet another price hike of up to 30 percent, driven by the insatiable demand for generative AI training hardware. The same GPUs that gamers want for ray tracing and high-fps 4K are being bought by the thousand for AI datacentres. The result: a market where an RTX 5090 costs more than a used car, and where the generative AI hardware crisis shows no signs of abating. The Valve RAM shortage story from last week, the Nvidia price hike today — hardware is becoming a luxury in an industry that was built on accessibility.

The Monkey Island creator Ron Gilbert has started production on Thimbleweed Park 2 — and it's due in 2028. 📺 Media

Clueless sequel series with Alicia Silverstone ordered at Paramount+

Variety · Hollywood Reporter

As if! Paramount+ has ordered a Clueless sequel series with Alicia Silverstone returning. The original 1995 film, directed by Amy Heckerling and based (loosely) on Jane Austen's Emma, is a genuine cultural artifact — the one that defined 90s teen cinema, invented half the slang your mum still uses ironically, and launched a thousand fashion trends. Silverstone's involvement is the key: without Cher Horowitz, there is no Clueless. The series format means we'll see where Cher is 30+ years later. I'm cautiously optimistic — legacy sequels are a minefield, but having the original star on board and a premise that's basically "what happens to the popular girl when she grows up" has real potential. Also: it's about time someone did a Clueless follow-up.

🎵 Music

Glen Hansard, Irish singer-songwriter and Once star, dies at 56

Pitchfork

Sad news this afternoon. Glen Hansard has died at 56, following a drinking-related seizure in March. The Irish singer-songwriter, best known as the frontman of The Frames and for his Oscar-winning work on the film Once (the song "Falling Slowly"), was a rare figure — someone who moved between indie credibility and mainstream success without ever seeming to compromise. His music was raw, emotional, deeply Irish in its melancholy and its defiance. Bono, Bruce Springsteen, and Cat Power have all paid tribute. The outpouring is genuine. He was one of those artists that other artists loved first, and the rest of us caught up to.

I first heard The Frames on a burned CD a friend brought back from Dublin in 2003. I'd never heard anything like it — that combination of fragile voice and wall-of-sound guitar, like someone shouting a secret. Hansard made music that felt like it was bleeding. The world is quieter tonight. Rest, Glen. Ar dheis Dé go raibh a anam.
